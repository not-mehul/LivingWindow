The Living Window

An open casement onto a small wild place. The land composes itself — no two hours are alike, and none will come again.

—
Open the window

Sound begins when you do. Headphones recommended — the land is spatial. Close it again and the hour is gone for good.

Generative. Every voice is synthesized in real time from a fresh seed. No recording, no loop — no session resembles the last.
Faithful. Each place keeps its own company: the blackbird's fluted phrases in the meadow, the curlew's rising cry over the shore, swifts screaming down a city street. Nothing sings where it would not live.
Peopled. Watch long enough and the land shows you more — a deer at the forest edge, sanderlings chasing the foam, a cat crossing the rooftops at midnight.
Unhurried. Voices take turns, as they do. Silence is part of the score.
Private. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
